## Why these are the top five

The inclusion threshold is score **15**, with irreversible safety/ordering harm winning ties. Scored omitted contenders are: data residency `2 x5 =10`, annual cost overrun `3 x3 =9`, category-manager fatigue `3 x3 =9`, and provider outage `2 x4 =8`. They remain controlled in the NFRs but rank below the five above. A contender is promoted when its score reaches 15 or evidence shows irreversible harm equal to R-04/R-05; one current row must then be demoted so the register still contains exactly five full risks.
