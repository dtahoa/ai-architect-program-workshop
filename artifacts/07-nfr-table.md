## Production gate

All rows are mandatory where their capability is in scope. Production replenishment influence requires every applicable critical condition to remain clear during representative shadow, three consecutive 6x tests, certified adapter/review drills, observed cost, and named operational coverage. Targets alone do not authorize production.
