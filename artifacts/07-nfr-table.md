## 3. Retry and deadline policy

- Partition count: 3,840. A normal partition averages `27M / 3,840 = 7,031` forecast outputs; the 6x test averages 42,188 forecast-equivalent records.
- Each stage operation is idempotent on `(run_id, partition_id, model_or_rules_version)`.
- Maximum two retries per failed partition, with 30-second then 120-second backoff plus jitter. A retry inherits the stage deadline; it does not reset it.
- No new internal retry starts after 01:30. No adapter retry starts after 01:55. The adapter is hard closed at 02:00.
- The retry reserve is 10 minutes. Acceptance assumes <=1% of partitions, or 39 of 3,840, need retry and can run in parallel. A higher rate triggers bypass rather than consuming MERLIN reserve.
- At 03:30, a recovered AI service remains closed. Recovery never creates a same-day write or rerun.

## 4. Production-influence release gates

All of the following are mandatory; failure of any gate means shadow-only:

1. Measured MERLIN P99 generation, native review and EDI time is <=90 minutes with a separate >=30-minute reserve to 04:00, or the AI hard close is moved earlier and the full capacity test is rerun.
2. Three consecutive 6x tests satisfy every stage floor, retry rule and cost threshold.
3. The SAP-certified override contract proves no pre-publication MERLIN-baseline read; documented post-generation queue fields/correlation; row ownership/manual protection; same-transaction epoch guard; supported staged <=5 s activation or measured direct P99 rate/overhead and cap formula; atomic rollback, locks/read timing and safe cleanup.
4. Kill-switch drills prove <=30 s acknowledgements, <=60 s rejection, <=5 min verified safe, deny-on-latch-loss/stale epoch, atomic check/write abort, independent credential/session revocation and separate exact-row cleanup without manual-row deletion.
5. Data completeness, residency, inventory-interval calibration, WAPE and audit-completeness gates pass on representative promotion and peak slices.
6. Native review-queue generated order ID/quantity/correlation fields, capacity and timeout/default behavior are certified. The pilot remains capped at 100 AI exceptions/night until then; missing fields keep influence disabled.
7. The $3.2M delivery basis and $3.8M recurring operating envelope are signed off separately by Finance.
8. Every operational and safety role has a named person and funded primary/backup coverage.

## 5. Validator decision

No prohibited design condition is present as corrected: MERLIN/POS remain unchanged; no current MERLIN result is invented pre-publication; MERLIN orders when AI fails; inventory is uncertain; numeric forecasting/optimization do not use an LLM; and displayed safety content is deterministic exact approved evidence/template or refusal, never generative. The throughput/cost envelopes are arithmetically feasible but the corrected adapter cap remains measured evidence.

However, production influence is **not approved** from documents alone. The decisive values - MERLIN P99 reserve, certified adapter behavior, observed 6x throughput/cost, review capacity, food-safety authority, residency field list, and Variant B staffing - are still unmeasured or unnamed. The design therefore **passes conditionally for historical replay and shadow mode only** and must be rejected for production influence until all eight gates pass.
