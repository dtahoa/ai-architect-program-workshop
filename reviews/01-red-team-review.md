# NovaMart Group 4 - Red-Team Architecture Review

> **Historical review record — superseded by [`final-review.md`](final-review.md).** Findings are retained for traceability; this file's original verdict is not the current verdict.

Reviewer role: Strict workshop trainer / adversarial architecture reviewer

Scope reviewed:

- `docs/workshop-1-ai-system-design.pdf`, all 6 rendered pages
- `artifacts/01-requirement-baseline.md` through `artifacts/14-final-artifact-pack.md`

Review standard: unsupported assumptions, cross-file contradictions, arithmetic, operational continuity, safety, compliance, cost completeness, genuine trade-offs, and seven-minute presentation feasibility. Planning targets are not treated as measured evidence.

## Executive review position

The ordering architecture has the right backbone: MERLIN remains authoritative, the AI sidecar is asynchronous and fail-open, POS and supplier EDI are isolated, shadow-assist is correctly treated as a rollout mode, and the 03:30 recovery case correctly remains closed. The artifacts are also unusually honest about unmeasured MERLIN timing, adapter semantics, Finance approval, staffing, and safety authority.

The pack is not yet ready for the workshop defense because one safety rule directly contradicts the request's rejection criteria, and four implementation/test gaps weaken the claims around supported integration, the kill switch, deadline arithmetic, and offline usefulness. These are correction issues in the artifacts, not reasons to weaken the conditional production gates.

## Findings

### RT-01 - Critical - The copilot still permits generative allergen and food-safety answers

**References:**

- `artifacts/02-architecture-decision-summary.md:47`
- `artifacts/04-c4-container.mmd:46`
- `artifacts/05-request-and-batch-flows.md:168`
- `artifacts/09-safety-and-governance.md:24`
- `artifacts/14-final-artifact-pack.md:226`
- Attached request, Agent 3 rejection rule: reject a design that gives uncited **or generative** allergen answers

**Impact:** A generated paraphrase can add, omit, or change a safety qualifier even when the cited passage is correct. This is exactly the fatal-harm and criminal-liability path the workshop says to reject. A post-generation citation verifier does not make the displayed answer non-generative, and a deterministic verifier cannot generally prove semantic entailment of arbitrary multilingual free text.

**Concrete evidence / reproduction:** Ask an exact allergen question while valid evidence exists. The online flow sends the evidence to a language service that may "summarize supplied passages" and then displays the summary if a verifier passes. The displayed safety statement was therefore generated by an LLM. This contradicts the pack's repeated claim that generative allergen answers are prohibited.

**Required correction:** Route allergen, food-safety, recall, sellability, storage, and shelf-life domains to deterministic extractive/template rendering only. The LLM may classify intent, extract candidate SKU/locale entities, or translate the user's query, but it must not compose the displayed safety answer. Render exact approved structured fields or approved passages with fixed wording and visible citations; otherwise refuse and escalate. Update the diagrams, request flow, safety policy, NFR, technology matrix, and consolidated pack consistently.

### RT-02 - Major - The pre-publication flow requires a current MERLIN baseline that no supported path supplies

**References:**

- `artifacts/04-c4-container.mmd:59`, `:77-81`
- `artifacts/05-request-and-batch-flows.md:38`, `:189`
- `artifacts/02-architecture-decision-summary.md:99`, `:149`
- `artifacts/09-safety-and-governance.md:182-184`

**Impact:** The sidecar cannot calculate the promised delta cap, risk rank, comparison, and audit baseline before it writes overrides unless it receives a current MERLIN min/max result through an undocumented interface or reimplements the legacy engine. The first option risks inventing a fourth MERLIN hook; the second duplicates 340,000 untested parameters and creates exactly the divergence the design says it avoids.

**Concrete evidence / reproduction:** Follow the container flow. Override rows flow `RECSTORE -> OVRADAPTER -> OVTABLE -> ENGINE`. The engine produces the current authoritative quantity only after it reads the override table. There is no `ENGINE -> sidecar` path before the 01:45 publish gate, yet the flow says the recommendation store compares every candidate with the MERLIN baseline before publication. The same sequencing gap exists for attaching context to an "AI-affected generated order": the review adapter has no shown post-generation order/reference input.

**Required correction:** Do not claim a current-run MERLIN baseline is available before override publication unless the certified nightly extension explicitly proves such an output without modifying engine sequencing. Remove pre-publication baseline-relative caps and comparisons otherwise. Use externally available absolute quantity/value bounds plus case-pack, shelf-life, DC-capacity, and inventory-interval constraints. Capture the true MERLIN generated quantity only after generation through the supported review-queue contract for review and audit. Show that post-generation join explicitly; if the queue contract cannot supply it, keep the comparison external/shadow-only and do not invent a read interface.

### RT-03 - Major - The kill switch has targets and actors, but not an executable fail-safe control path

**References:**

- `artifacts/02-architecture-decision-summary.md:124-130`
- `artifacts/05-request-and-batch-flows.md:120-125`
- `artifacts/06-legacy-assessment.md:132-152`
- `artifacts/09-safety-and-governance.md:200-205`
- `artifacts/14-final-artifact-pack.md:477`

**Impact:** Setting a named variable in a control plane does not prove that a cached or partitioned adapter will reject a write within one minute. The same adapter that may be faulty is also expected to perform cleanup. A control-plane outage, stale `ENABLED` state, or check/write race can leave publication active while the artifacts claim safe state.

**Concrete evidence / reproduction:** Start an adapter publication, isolate the adapter from the control plane after it has cached `ENABLED`, and then have an operator set `AI_OVERRIDE_PUBLISH=DISABLED`. No artifact specifies the authoritative state store, monotonic version/epoch, maximum cache age, acknowledgement quorum, an atomic check inside the MERLIN transaction, or an independent way to revoke the adapter's write capability. The <=1 minute and <=5 minute values are therefore objectives, not an executable design.

**Required correction:** Define a durable on-premises, deny-by-default publication latch with a monotonic version that the adapter validates inside the same transaction as every write. Unreachable, expired, unsigned, or non-current control state must reject publication. Define acknowledgement and verification evidence. Add an independent SAP Operations stop, such as revoking the adapter's MERLIN write credential or disabling its narrowly scoped network/DB access, plus a separate certified exact-row cleanup runbook. Test control-plane loss, stale adapter state, check/write races, adapter compromise/failure, and cleanup failure.

### RT-04 - Major - Adapter publication arithmetic uses a 15-minute rate while imposing a 60-second transaction lock

**References:**

- `artifacts/08-capacity-and-cost-check.md:40`, `:93`
- `artifacts/07-nfr-table.md`, VA-04 and NFR-01/NFR-11 release gates
- `artifacts/12-risk-register.md`, R-04
- `artifacts/14-final-artifact-pack.md`, NFR-01 and R-04

**Impact:** The controlled-expansion cap and deadline proof can be overstated. If all 70,400 rows must be inserted atomically while the override-table transaction is locked for at most 60 seconds, the required in-transaction rate is much higher than the documented rate. Missing this can extend a MERLIN lock, force partial publication, or consume the incumbent deadline reserve.

**Concrete evidence / reproduction:** The capacity table divides `70,400 / 900 = 78.2 rows/s`, then states preparation at 313 rows/s and a certified commit lock <=60 seconds. Preparation outside MERLIN does not insert the rows into MERLIN. The actual one-transaction write requirement is `70,400 / 60 = 1,173.3 rows/s`, excluding commit/checksum overhead. For the 5,000-row pilot it is at least `83.3 rows/s` inside the certified lock.

**Required correction:** Add the in-transaction write/commit rate and overhead to the acceptance arithmetic. Set the row cap from measured contract capacity, for example `floor(certified safe rows/s x certified lock seconds)` with headroom, rather than selecting 1% first. Do not assume a staging table, atomic swap, or bulk interface unless it is part of the existing supported override-table contract. Propagate the corrected cap/rate through NFR, risk, ADR, Variant B, and final-pack evidence.

### RT-05 - Major - The offline copilot acceptance test can pass while refusing every question

**References:**

- `artifacts/05-request-and-batch-flows.md:170`
- `artifacts/07-nfr-table.md`, NFR-07
- `artifacts/08-capacity-and-cost-check.md:211`
- `artifacts/14-final-artifact-pack.md:254`

**Impact:** A 100% refusal system can satisfy the current metric, so the design does not prove that the copilot "works" offline or contributes to reducing lookup time below 15 minutes per shift. Safe refusal is essential for unsupported safety queries, but it is not a functional success for eligible planogram, SOP, or product-knowledge questions.

**Concrete evidence / reproduction:** Configure an empty but valid signed pack. Every query returns a safe refusal under the latency limit. The metric "at least 95% yield cited evidence or safe refusal" passes, despite providing no usable lookup answers.

**Required correction:** Split the acceptance measures. For eligible non-safety benchmark questions, require a high percentage of correct cited answers, with coverage and accuracy measured separately. For safety cases, require 100% policy correctness: an exact deterministic answer only when eligible evidence exists and a correct refusal/escalation for stale, missing, conflicting, or invalid evidence. Count refusal as success only where the benchmark policy expects refusal. Keep latency and signed-pack availability as separate SLOs.

### RT-06 - Minor - Technology scores are not reproducible enough to prove the weights were not outcome-driven

**References:**

- `artifacts/10-technology-selection-matrix.md:7-12`
- `artifacts/10-technology-selection-matrix.md`, Decisions A-D
- `artifacts/14-final-artifact-pack.md`, Artifact 3

**Impact:** The narrative trade-offs are real, but a trainer cannot independently reproduce why a score is 3 rather than 4. This is most exposed in the close copilot result: governed retrieval with an LLM scores 4.55 versus deterministic keyword/search at 4.25, despite the safety and simplicity concerns.

**Concrete evidence / reproduction:** Re-score the copilot cost/support or evidence-safety criterion down by one point and the apparent margin changes materially. The pack defines the meaning of 1 and 5 only generically and provides no criterion-specific anchors or sensitivity result.

**Recommended correction:** Add short 1/3/5 anchors for each consequential criterion and a one-line evidence rationale for disputed scores. Show a simple sensitivity check for Decisions A and D. After RT-01, re-score deterministic extractive safety behavior separately from non-safety language assistance.

### RT-07 - Minor - The register does not demonstrate why these are the top five risks

**References:**

- `artifacts/12-risk-register.md:5-17`
- `artifacts/14-final-artifact-pack.md:407-419`

**Impact:** Every selected risk has a signal and numeric threshold, but the "top five" claim is not traceable against omitted contenders. Cost overrun, residency breach, and review overload are material and could tie or exceed a score of 15 under the unresolved assumptions.

**Concrete evidence / reproduction:** The register shows inherent scores only for the five selected rows. There is no scored candidate disposition that explains why cost, residency, or reviewer fatigue fell below R-04/R-05, nor a tie-break rule.

**Recommended correction:** Keep exactly five full risk rows, but add a compact selection note listing the next three contenders with likelihood, impact, score, and the tie-break reason. Do not turn the contender note into additional full register entries.

## Cross-file consistency and arithmetic notes

- **Contradiction requiring correction:** The pack says generative safety answers are prohibited while five artifacts allow LLM summarization of safety evidence. See RT-01.
- **Sequencing contradiction requiring correction:** The pre-generation recommendation store claims a current MERLIN baseline even though the diagrams show the engine produces results only after override-table read. See RT-02.
- **Arithmetic correction required:** 70,400 rows in a <=60-second atomic lock requires at least 1,173.3 rows/s, not the 78.2 rows/s 15-minute floor used in the capacity row. See RT-04.
- **No material arithmetic error found** in the 27M/7.04M reconciliation, 162M/42.24M conservative peak, stage-precision `198.66 -> 198.7`-minute sum and `41.34 -> 41.3`-minute headroom, annual compute total, $3.31242M subtotal, $3.8M target, cost-per-forecast values, or net-margin/ROI ratios.
